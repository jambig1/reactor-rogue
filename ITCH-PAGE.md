## Page description

**Reactor Rogue** is a survivors-like space shooter for browser and mobile. Your weapons fire on their own; you steer, dash and choose upgrades as the swarm grows, then face the reactor bosses that guard each sector.

**Build your ship**
- Five equipment slots (engine, wings, gun mount, plating, reactor) and 31 parts across six rarities, from Common to sector-only Exotics.
- Epic parts and above carry signature mechanics: dashes that leave a burning wake, a siege lock that rewards standing your ground, armour that grows stronger the deeper you wade in.
- Honest comparisons: every part shows exactly what you gain and lose before you fit it.
- Every fitting appears on your ship in the 3D hangar and in flight.

**Grow your pilot**
- Four class branches: Interceptor, Juggernaut, Gunship, Carrier. Each has techniques that change how you fight, plus mastery ranks once the tree is complete.
- Crew, workshop upgrades, named loadouts and a collection to complete.

**Make it yours**
- Paint schemes you earn by playing, accent colours, Gloss, Satin, Matte and Chrome finishes, and ten squadron markings.

**Play anywhere**
- Keyboard, gamepad or touch, with touch buttons you can move. Saves stay on your device, with backup and restore.

## Devlog: 1.7.0 — The ship update

The biggest update so far is all about your ship.

- **One ship hub** for equipment, skills, crew, workshop, appearance, loadouts and collection, with short tips the first time you open each view.
- **Six rarity tiers and 11 new parts**, including sector-only Exotics that drop from that sector's bosses, and a reveal when you find one.
- **A fifth slot for reactor cores.** Your old reactor module carries over automatically.
- **Skill techniques and masteries**: a new tier in every class branch that changes how you play, and five mastery ranks to keep levelling worthwhile.
- **Cosmetics**: earned paint schemes, finishes, accent colours and squadron markings. Your in-flight ship now looks like the hangar model (Classic neon is still an option).
- **Test flight**: try your exact build against waves or a boss with nothing at stake.
- Balance and save fixes. Full notes are in the changelog.

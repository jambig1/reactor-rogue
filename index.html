FIELD MANUAL

Your first run

Move, collect XP gems and choose an upgrade at each level. Defeat the reactor bosses at 3, 6 and 10 minutes, then survive the final encounter to clear the sector.

Flying and firing

PC: WASD or arrows to move, Shift for your ship ability, P or Esc to pause. Touch: drag to steer and use the ability button. Weapons fire automatically unless you select Manual starter in Settings.

Basics

Launch cards

After you press Launch, you are dealt three launch cards. Pick one to bend the rules for that run, or fly without one. Cards: Glass Reactor (+45% damage, −40% max hull), Iron Hull (+60 max hull, −15% damage), Critical Mass (+15% critical chance, critical hits ×3), Boss Hunter (+70% damage to bosses and their systems), Lucky Cache (cache bonuses and jackpots far more often), Overclocked Thrusters (abilities recharge 40% faster), Second Wind (survive one killing blow at 30% hull), Scrap Baron (+50% credits) and Gravity Collector (pickup radius ×2.5, +20% XP). Your card shows on the pause screen. Keys 1–3 pick a card.

Building your arsenal

You have six weapon slots and six support slots. Your first level offers a starter upgrade, and equipped weapons remain available for regular upgrades. Raise a weapon to level 5, collect its matching support and open an elite cache to evolve it. Pause → Evolutions shows the exact requirements and your progress. Skips pass on an upgrade; rerolls replace choices; banishes remove an offered item from that run.

Levels and endless survival

Every level needs more XP than the last. Later levels rise more steeply, while spare XP carries over. After a sector clear you can finish or choose endless. Endless enemy health and damage rise continuously, with a new cycle and returning sector reactor every two game minutes. Spawn frequency rises up to twice its initial endless rate; existing ship and projectile limits remain. Strong defences and abilities still work, but enemy damage keeps growing. Your original sector clear remains banked. Five minutes into endless, Reactor Overdrive starts: hordes of weak orange drones pour in, and every weapon hit is multiplied (×1.5 after a minute, rising each minute to a ×7 cap).

Finding pickups

Credit drops use round coins, supplies use hexagonal badges and evolution caches use gold square cases. Important supplies have readable names and stay above decorative explosions. Edge arrows point to caches, repair, freeze, purge and XP vacuum drops; nearby arrows are grouped to reduce clutter. Expiring drops show their last five seconds instead of blinking away. Pickup markers can be disabled in Gameplay settings.

Supply and evolution caches

Caches are classified when they drop. Cyan supply caches give repair, XP or a temporary boost. Gold evolution caches only drop when a weapon is level 5 and its matching support is owned. One evolution cache can wait at a time; other cache drops remain supplies. Supply caches do not change into evolutions later. If a recipe is removed in developer testing, a waiting evolution cache becomes supplies before collection. Every cache also rolls a bonus tier as it opens: BONUS adds a free level to one of your items and some XP; JACKPOT levels up to three items, fully repairs your hull and grants 10 seconds of Overcharge. Evolution caches roll bonuses far more often than supply caches. Tap during the reveal to skip it.

Ships, credits and permanent upgrades

Sovereign is the premium twelfth ship: defeat 20 sector bosses across runs, then buy its 1,500-credit licence. It starts with Void Seeds, gains 4% weapon damage per evolution (24% cap), and blinks with a short-lived gravity rift. It has 125 HP, 1 armour and a 10-second ability cooldown.

Vanguard starts free. Complete a ship challenge, then buy its licence in Hangar → Ships. Earlier saves retain the original six ships. Credits come from kills, survival, bosses, breaches and contracts, even on defeat. Buy permanent extra skips, rerolls and banishes in Upgrade tools. Changes apply to your next run.

Achievements and contracts

The Hangar Contracts page tracks 31 goals across blueprints, milestones, combat, mastery and exploration. Rewards range from 50 to 400 one-off credits, paid when progress is banked. Existing profiles receive the difference for previously paid achievements once when loaded. Completed goals stay as earned badges in that profile. Older ready goals can be claimed from the board. In-run notices show new achievements; the results screen lists rewards paid. Developer tests never advance them.

Saves and records

Progress stays in this browser on this device. Runs are recorded on defeat, victory, restart or return to start. Opening the Hangar while paused banks your progress. Weapon damage and kills appear in Pause → Weapons and results.

Combat

Reading combat threats

Hostile shots have a warm diamond outline and bright centre. Attack warnings and hostile shots stay above explosions and damage numbers. Warm dashed hazard rings mean an attack is preparing; solid rings mark active danger. Small pale corner marks help locate your ship; they are a locator, not a hitbox. Damage numbers move clear of that marker. These cues stay visible on Low graphics and with reduced motion.

Enemy formations

Fighter squadrons begin around 0:18, crossing attack lanes from 1:05, heavy escorts from 1:50, encirclements from 2:30, pincers from 3:40 and swarm bursts from 4:40. Patterns mix with ordinary pursuit. Edge arrows give 1.6 seconds of warning. Attack lanes have a wide gap; encirclements leave a marked opening. Ships hold their approach briefly then return to pursuit. Formation ships replace future ordinary arrivals, stay within existing heavy/population caps, and are held during boss fights. Swarm bursts are followed by a recovery period. Formation timing and state reset each run.

Priority enemy ships

Hunters arrive after 50 seconds: their pale dashed lane locks before a short charge. Volatile ships arrive after 80 seconds; their death blast damages nearby enemies after a short orange warning and can set off other volatile ships. Shield carriers arrive after 105 seconds: blue rings mark allies with 20% extra damage reduction; destroy or freeze the carrier to remove it. Fighter carriers arrive after 145 seconds: glowing side bays warn before two small fighters launch. Each carrier can launch at most 12 fighters, with six alive at once. New specialist arrivals use the existing spawn budget and pause during recovery windows and boss encounters. Freeze stops their abilities. No extra evolution caches are awarded.

Enemy pressure and performance

Ordinary purple brutes are limited to eight alive, separate from specialist ships. At the limit, regular spawns use lighter ships. Yellow elites are limited to two alive and use a single health curve; each still drops its evolution cache and 50 XP. A blocked elite arrival retries after five seconds, without stacking overdue arrivals. Bosses retain their own health and mechanics.

Auto graphics immediately suppress glow and simplify ice decoration when fights become crowded. Attack warnings, silhouettes, terrain boundaries and your damage-number preference remain visible. Auto also lowers detail sooner when measured rendering is slow. Low, Medium, High and Custom retain your manual choices. These cosmetic changes do not affect damage, movement, drops or game time.

Post-reactor recovery

After a reactor falls, ordinary reinforcements ease for eight game seconds so you can collect rewards. Existing enemies and attacks remain dangerous. Combat and this timer pause on reward screens. The next scheduled boss still arrives on time.

Varied operations and field protocols

Fleet operations are shuffled each run, with a recovery window every fourth operation. Optional Relay Circuits ask you to hold three nodes for 1.25 seconds each within 45 seconds. Complete a circuit to choose one run-only damage protocol: close range, long range, cold targets or siege. A new protocol replaces your current one and uses no equipment slot.

Optional distress signals

One optional encounter appears at a time, starting after 75 seconds. Follow the HUD arrow and stay in the marked zone for three continuous game seconds to start automatically. Leaving resets the countdown. Ignore or dismiss an unstarted signal without penalty. Reactor breaches auto-start at standard risk; Risk options still lets you choose a harder breach before it starts. Boss fights suspend signal timers, freighter damage and reactor damage.

Stranded Pilot: board the pilot within 65m for 3 seconds, then reach the extraction gate within 50 seconds. Freighter SOS: defend within 130m for 22 seconds before its hull or 55-second timer runs out. Unstable Reactor: destroy the marked core within 35 seconds; remote containment seals it if you fail. Enemy waves are warned for one second and capped at twelve extra ships per encounter, within the existing 240-ship limit.

Each success gives 20 XP, 300 points and one choice: a compatible Rare-or-better upgrade (40 XP if none fit), 45 HP repair (one run reroll if already healthy), or 25 credits. Credits bank normally. These signals do not count as reactor breaches for existing unlocks. The three signals appear in a shuffled cycle followed by an original reactor breach. Active signals can be abandoned; failures lose the reward but do not end your run.

Alien merchant & scrap

The Lost Signal offers three random exclusive modifications around 2:00, 5:00 and 8:00. The merchant transmission opens automatically when he arrives and pauses combat. End the transmission to return to the fight. Bosses hold arrivals. The transmission stays open until you close it; closing ends that visit. Every fourth ordinary kill drops 3 scrap; elites drop 12 and bosses 30. Scrap expires after 90s on the floor. Collect it nearby, or use a Salvage Drone. Scrap is separate from permanent Hangar credits. All fifteen modifications last only this run and use no slots. Owned items leave the stock pool. One free refresh per visit; further refreshes cost 15, 25, 35… scrap. Installed effects appear in Pause → Overview. Unspent scrap is lost at the end.

Salvage drops and rates

Salvage crates: 10% spawn check each game second, increased by Luck to 50%, with up to 10 crates nearby. Break a crate for one weighted drop. Ordinary ships drop XP; elites occasionally drop combat supplies or passive upgrades. Bosses and elites keep their evolution caches.

Common: Credit Chip (+1), Credit Bundle (+5), Repair Cell (+30 HP). Rare: Credit Cache (+25, level 5), Chrono Stop (10s, level 4; bosses 2s), Purge Beacon (level 8, shockwave destroys all visible ships, including bosses), XP Vacuum (level 12, gems only), Plasma Breath (10s aimed cone), Fortune Shard (+10% Luck this run), Reroll Token (+1 this run). Level 30: Salvage Fever (10s, +1 credit per 5 kills, max 20) and ultra-rare Reactor Surge (14s invulnerability and fast fire; 50 kills earns a cache, otherwise +5 credits).

Weights before Luck: Chips 50, Bundles 10, Repair 12, Chrono 2, Vacuum 2, Cache 1, Purge 1, Plasma 1, Fortune 0.5, Reroll 1, Fever 1, Surge 0.02. Level gates remove unavailable drops before rolling. Loot remains for 120s. Credits bank on defeat or leaving; purchased tool allowances are unchanged.

Arsenal

Expanded reactor arsenal

48 weapons and evolutions. New equipment includes orbiting Ion Guardians, lingering Plasma Rain, reflecting Prism Tracers, arcing Comet Scythes, vertical Ion Curtain, Coronal Shroud, alternating Plasma Lash, Binary Wisps, Storm Spires, Echo Rings, Crossfire Array and Rupture Drill. Each has two level-3 specialisations. Six extra support items improve ability recovery, shield recharge, reactive slowing, repairs, elite damage or low-hull damage. Six weapon and six support slots still apply. Evolution hints appear only on the missing partner, using this run’s equipment.

Expanded arsenal and defence

Ten extra weapons: Rail Lance, Flak Battery, Crescent Blades, Plasma Fences, Leech Beam, Cryo Charges, Kinetic Ram, Solar Sails, Void Seeds and Stasis Net. Each has a matching-support evolution and two level-3 specialisations. There are now 36 weapon recipes. Six weapon slots and six support slots remain.

Four defensive supports: Ablative Plates absorb damage and regenerate; Reactive Screen grants brief damage reduction after a hit; Decoy Beacon leaves a distraction when you use a ship ability; Salvage Medics repair hull from credit pickups. Four passive supports: Supply Tractor pulls supplies but not XP, Guidance Chip improves speed and homing, Field Capacitor extends planted fields, and Salvage Appraiser increases currency pickup value. All are run-only upgrades with five levels. Their exact effects and level changes appear on upgrade cards.

Weapon specialisations

At level 3, Neon Bolts, Scatterburst, Frost Shards, Chain Lightning, Toxic Cloud, Gravity Well, Nova Reactor, Pulse Laser, Siege Cannon, Needle Repeater and Energy Shield each offer two paths. The ten expansion weapons listed above also branch at level 3. Combat pauses while you choose. Press 1–2 or tap a card. The choice uses no equipment slot, lasts for this run and carries through evolution. It resets when you start again.

Choose extra projectiles or penetration, focused or wider cones, stronger freezes or forward ice javelins, long lightning chains or three concentrated strikes, trailing or concentrated poison, stronger gravity pull or early collapse, wide or double novas, and broad or armour-cutting lasers. Each card explains its trade-off. Pause shows the full chosen effects; upgrade cards and weapon statistics show the selected path.

Sectors

Sectors and bosses

Play opens ship selection, followed by sector selection. Choose an owned ship and an unlocked sector, then launch. Your last choices are remembered. The Hangar contains ship licences, upgrades, contracts and loadouts. Each sector has its own bosses. Watch attack warnings, avoid marked salvage drops and arc strikes, and destroy power relays to reduce storm boss armour. Clear contracts to unlock more sectors and equipment.

Interactive sector features

Neon Expanse keeps its neon arena and gains derelict stations, cargo cover and salvage pockets. Shattered Belt favours asteroid cover and salvage. Storm Relay has overloadable conduits, ion currents and stations. Features begin after 18 seconds, with a new opportunity about every 40 seconds when space permits.

Cover blocks physical friendly and hostile projectiles and can be destroyed. Energy beams and area attacks bypass it. Dash into cover to damage it; phase abilities pass through. Small ships steer around it, and a charging hunter stops on impact. Cover stays solid during boss fights.

Approach a derelict station and tap Dock or press F. Stay within 85m for 4 seconds while combat continues to repair 45 HP. Leaving cancels docking. Each station has one charge; up to three spawn in a normal run. A full hull does not consume the charge.

Salvage pockets slow ships by 20% and contain three 5-credit pods. Supply Tractor can draw them out. Their pickups use normal repair, appraisal and banking rules. Up to four pockets spawn in a normal run.

Shoot a conduit or approach and press F to prime it. A 1.4-second warning marks its 150m blast. It deals 180 damage to enemies and 22 to you, so move clear or use invulnerability. Ion currents push ordinary ships along their marked direction. Boss encounters suspend docking, conduit charging/countdowns, salvage collection and currents. All terrain, charges and limits reset each run.

Sector fleets

Shattered Belt mixes rugged Scrap Marauder hulls with the familiar fleet. Storm Relay adds crystalline Relay Sentinel hulls alongside the usual ships. Their outlines retain the enemy strength colours; health, armour, rewards and population limits are unchanged. Each sector keeps its existing distinct bosses.

New frontier sectors

Cinder Foundry and Cryo Reach unlock after Rogue operator, your first sector clear. Choose them in Play → Choose sector. Foundry has furnace platforms, industrial ships and short rectangular heat vents; Cryo Reach has fractured ice shelves, crystal ships and narrow frost fractures. Warm dashed lanes warn before damage and solid warm borders mark active zones. Boss attacks at 3:00, 6:00 and 10:00 keep ordinary environmental hazards on hold. Each sector has three unique bosses. Existing population, equipment, rewards and ten-minute clear rules remain. New sectors use the existing techno and jungle soundtrack respectively.

Neon Undercity

A rooftop city arena with neon signs, service roads and distant traffic. Buildings are scenery below your ship, not solid obstacles; marked cover remains physical. Security lockdown lanes warn before activating. Patrol Interdictor fires a tight triple spread and aimed lane; Skyline Enforcer uses twin cannon banks and paired avenues; Metroplex Overlord fires a ring with an escape gap and a four-lane blockade with a safe central pocket. Choose Play → Choose sector after your first sector clear.
